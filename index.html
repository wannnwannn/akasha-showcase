Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really 100% free? How do you make money?

Zero ads, zero hidden fees. This is an open-source project built out of passion, not profit. It's AGPL-3.0 licensed, so you can even peek under the hood yourself.

Are you selling my personal data?

Absolutely not. Your lists, notes, and watch history stay yours. This is a personal tracker, not an ad network. We don't collect, share, or monetize your data.

Where do the movies, anime, and books in search come from?

We pull from the best global sources in real time. TMDB for film, AniList for Asian pop culture, and Hardcover for literature. No stale archives, just fresh, reliable metadata.

What if I want to track something niche or just a random video?

No problem! Add it manually, or paste a YouTube URL and import it instantly. If it exists, you can track it.

Can I import my history from MyAnimeList, Letterboxd, or other?

Yes, you can. The import feature works with several platforms, and updates are being made to improve it.

Is there an iOS or Android app?

Not a native one yet. But this is a full-featured PWA web app. Just add it to your home screen, and it works like an app, with no download or store required.
